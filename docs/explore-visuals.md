# Explore: a living river valley

Replace the tunnel with an open river valley. Similar tracks are groves on
branching footpaths; the aimed grove is a destination, and its smaller offshoots
preview the next choices. A dive moves into a new, seeded stretch of country.
The river gives the mix a continuous direction without enclosing the listener.

The tunnel's rings, black void and speed bursts suggest machinery. A literal
tree or mycelium would explain the graph well, but crowd its readable labels
and offer less room for sky, weather and wildlife. The valley keeps the tree's
relationships while giving the music a believable place to inhabit.

Low is an ochre grassland, mid a green riverside grove, high an open blue-grey
wetland. Light drifts from morning toward evening as music plays; grasses grow,
wind follows smoothed audio energy, and birds and butterflies arrive gradually.
No beat flashes or bloom. Reduced motion holds the camera and wildlife still,
with slow changes in light and vegetation.

The four waveforms are the river's foreground banks and reflected contours.
Their horizontal axis remains linear (four seconds either side of now), with
aligned beat/bar ticks, loop brackets and a filled focused deck. Mineral blue,
ochre, pale limestone and heather identify the decks; numbered keys and distinct
line patterns provide identity without relying on hue alone. Controls remain
readable on dark earth surfaces, above the landscape.

Everything is generated locally from geometry and shaders. No external assets,
runtime asset downloads or backend/protocol changes are needed.
